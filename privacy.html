Privacy Policy

This application is a private Home Assistant installation operated by a single individual for personal home automation. It has no other users.

Data accessed

The application accesses only the Google Drive and Google Sheets data of the account that authorises it - that is, the owner's own account. It uses this access to upload backup files and to append home energy-consumption records.

Data sharing

No data is transmitted to any third party. No data is sold, shared or used for advertising or analytics. All processing happens on hardware located in the owner's home.

Data retention

Files created by the application remain in the owner's own Google account and are managed by the owner. Revoking access at myaccount.google.com/permissions immediately stops all access.
